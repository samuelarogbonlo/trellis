Common Failure Modes

Why 91% of users abandon gas abstraction after one try

Invalid Nonce Errors

42% of failures

Transaction nonce conflicts cause the most common failures across providers.

Solutions:

  • Implement proper nonce management in your app
  • Use provider-specific nonce tracking
  • Add retry logic with exponential backoff
Affects: Biconomy, Pimlico most frequently

Insufficient Funds

28% of failures

Users underestimate gas costs due to hidden fees and overhead.

Solutions:

  • Show real costs upfront including fees
  • Implement cost estimation with 20% buffer
  • Provide clear error messages about actual requirements
Affects: All providers, especially on Ethereum

Network Timeouts

18% of failures

Provider bundler congestion causes transaction timeouts.

Solutions:

  • Implement multi-provider fallback
  • Set appropriate timeout values (30s+)
  • Provide real-time status updates to users
Affects: Gelato and Biconomy more frequently
